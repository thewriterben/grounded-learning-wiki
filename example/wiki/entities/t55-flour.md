# T55 flour

A refined French white flour, the usual choice for baguettes.[^use]

- Ash content: 0.50 to 0.60 percent.[^ash]
- Compare [[t65-flour]], which is a little less refined.

[^ash]: raw/flour-types-note.md "T55 flour has an ash content of 0.50 to 0.60 percent"
