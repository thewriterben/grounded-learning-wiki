# TRAPPIST-1e

A rocky planet of a cool red dwarf, in its star's [[habitable-zone]].[^hz]

- Radius: about 0.92 Earth radii.[^e]
- Orbital period: about 6.1 days.[^e]
- Its outer neighbour is [[trappist-1f]].

[^e]: raw/trappist-1-note.md "TRAPPIST-1e has a radius of about 0.92 Earth radii and an orbital period of about 6.1 days"
[^hz]: raw/hz-note.md "TRAPPIST-1e, TRAPPIST-1f and Kepler-452b are all thought to orbit within their star's habitable zone"
