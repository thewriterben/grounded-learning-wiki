# Kepler-452b

A planet of a Sun-like star,[^star] in its star's [[habitable-zone]].[^hz] Found by the
[[transit-method]].

- Radius: about 1.6 Earth radii.[^b]
- Orbital period: about 385 days.[^b]

[^b]: raw/kepler-452b-note.md "Kepler-452b has a radius of about 1.6 Earth radii and an orbital period of about 385 days"
[^star]: raw/kepler-452b-note.md "Kepler-452b orbits a star much like the Sun"
[^hz]: raw/hz-note.md "TRAPPIST-1e, TRAPPIST-1f and Kepler-452b are all thought to orbit within their star's habitable zone"
