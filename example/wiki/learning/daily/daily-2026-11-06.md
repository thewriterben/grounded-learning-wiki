# Daily guide, 6 November

**The days in one paragraph.** Two notes went in, one on French flour types and one on feeding a
levain, plus the house loaf's recipe log. The wiki now has four pages.

**Concept of the day: ash content.** The T number on a French flour is its ash content. Burn the flour
and weigh what is left: that is the mineral part, most of it from the outer layers of the grain. More
ash means a less refined, darker, more flavourful flour.

**Numbers.** T55 sits at 0.50 to 0.60 percent ash ([[t55-flour]]) and T65 at 0.62 to 0.75 percent
([[t65-flour]]). A levain fed 1:5:5 peaks in about 8 to 12 hours ([[levain]]).
