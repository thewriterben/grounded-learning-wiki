# Daily guide, 6 November

**The days in one paragraph.** Two notes on planets went in, then notes on the transit method, the
habitable zone and the light-curve pipeline. The wiki now has seven pages.

**Concept of the day: the transit method.** When a planet passes in front of its star, the star dims a
little. How much it dims tells you how big the planet is next to the star; how often it dims tells you
how long the planet's year is.

**Numbers.** TRAPPIST-1e is about 0.92 Earth radii with a year of about 6.1 days ([[trappist-1e]]).
Kepler-452b is about 1.6 Earth radii with a year of about 385 days ([[kepler-452b]]).
