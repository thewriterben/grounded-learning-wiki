# Note on the habitable zone

Illustrative text written for this demo. The figures are rounded and published values get revised, so check a current catalogue before using them.

The habitable zone is the range of distances from a star at which a rocky planet could keep liquid
water on its surface.
TRAPPIST-1e, TRAPPIST-1f and Kepler-452b are all thought to orbit within their star's habitable zone.
