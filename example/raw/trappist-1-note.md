# Note on the TRAPPIST-1 system

Illustrative text written for this demo. The figures are rounded and published values get revised, so check a current catalogue before using them.

TRAPPIST-1 is a cool red dwarf star with seven known rocky planets.
TRAPPIST-1e has a radius of about 0.92 Earth radii and an orbital period of about 6.1 days.
TRAPPIST-1f has a radius of about 1.04 Earth radii and an orbital period of about 9.2 days.
