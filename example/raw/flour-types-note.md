# Note on French flour types

Illustrative example text written for this demo. It is not a real datasheet; do not bake from it.

French flours are named by their ash content, the mineral residue left when the flour is burned.
T55 flour has an ash content of 0.50 to 0.60 percent. T65 flour has an ash content of 0.62 to 0.75
percent. T80 flour has an ash content of 0.75 to 0.90 percent.

T55 is the usual flour for baguettes. T65 is the usual flour for country loaves.
