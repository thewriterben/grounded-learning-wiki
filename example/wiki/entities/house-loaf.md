# House loaf

The everyday loaf in this kitchen, made with [[t65-flour]] and a [[levain]]. The recipe has versions,
so a question about it has to say which one.

| Version | Hydration |
|---|---|
| v1 | 70 percent[^v1] |
| v2 | 75 percent, with a longer cold proof[^v2] |
