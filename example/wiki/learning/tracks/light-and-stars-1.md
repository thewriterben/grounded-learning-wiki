# Light and stars 1: why a star dims

A track lesson for days with no activity. A star's brightness can drop because something crosses in
front of it, because of dark spots on its surface, or because the star itself pulses. Only the first is
a transit ([[transit-method]]); the second is why the pipeline removes star-spot trends ([[pipeline]]).
